# WaterProof usability check (2–3 participants)

**Status: protocol only. No participants, timings or outcomes have been collected; usability is not validated.**

Recruit 2–3 people who work with environmental or remote-sensing data (record their experience; do not assume SAR
expertise). Use anonymous IDs P1–P3, get consent before recording, and test the same app version for everyone.

## Task (read aloud, 10–15 minutes)

> "Open WaterProof, select a research example, identify the relevant location and observation dates, explain the main
> uncertainty, and export an investigation note."

Moderator script: "Please think aloud. We are testing the application, not you. The results are research results; the app
does not confirm flooding. I will not explain the controls first." Start on the home page (Explore the world). Do not
reveal the answers below.

## Observable success criteria

| # | Criterion | Counts as completed when the participant… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Candidate / example selection completed | opens the guided Thailand example or a Louisiana candidate without help |
| 2 | Location correctly identified | names the place (Prachin Buri, Thailand, or the Louisiana candidate's coordinates) and points to it on a map |
| 3 | Dates correctly identified | states both NISAR acquisition dates of the selected pair (not the reference-product dates) |
| 4 | Main uncertainty understood | states the shown main uncertainty in their own words (e.g. the pre-event control or date dependence) and that results do not confirm flooding |
| 5 | Report successfully exported | downloads the review note and confirms its location, dates and next step match the screen |

Follow-up questions: (1) Which wording or control was confusing? (2) Did anything imply certainty or confirmed flooding?
Where? (3) What was missing from the exported note? (4) Which evidence scope label did you notice, and what did it mean
to you?

## Answer guide (moderator only)

- Guided example: Thailand, Prachin Buri, NISAR track 004 D, 2026-09-06 → 2026-09-30; scope "case-specific evaluation";
  main caution: the pre-event control (6 → 18 Sep) still produced 3.14 km² of new-water candidates.
- Louisiana candidate 1: seed 2026-07-03 → 2026-07-15, about −90.26474, 29.91097 (lon, lat); main limitation: the seed
  change is not notable without 15 July; scope "Louisiana research exploration".

Report individual outcomes; 2–3 participants cannot establish general effectiveness. Fix observed blockers before
adding features. Keep raw notes separately, with consent.

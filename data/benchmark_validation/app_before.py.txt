def radar_plot(array,kind):
    fig,ax=plt.subplots(figsize=(7,3.7),layout='constrained')
    west,south,east,north=array_bounds(*array.shape,data['grid']['transform'])
    cmap=plt.get_cmap('RdBu_r' if kind=='difference' else 'gray').copy();cmap.set_bad(colors[0])
    if kind=='classes':
        opts=dict(cmap=ListedColormap(colors),norm=BoundaryNorm(np.arange(-.5,5.5),5))
    elif kind=='difference':
        limit=max(1.,float(np.percentile(np.abs(result['delta'][data['valid']]),98)))
        opts=dict(cmap=cmap,vmin=-limit,vmax=limit)
    else:
        lo,hi=np.percentile(np.concatenate([data['before'][data['valid']],data['after'][data['valid']]]),[2,98])
        opts=dict(cmap=cmap,vmin=lo,vmax=max(hi,lo+.01))
    im=ax.imshow(array,extent=(west,east,south,north),origin='upper',interpolation='nearest',**opts)
    ax.set_xlabel('Easting (m)',fontsize=8);ax.set_ylabel('Northing (m)',fontsize=8)
    ax.ticklabel_format(useOffset=False,style='plain');ax.tick_params(labelsize=7)
    if kind!='classes':fig.colorbar(im,ax=ax,shrink=.8,label='Δ dB' if kind=='difference' else 'Gamma-0 (dB)')
    return fig

We intersect valid positive-power masks, apply a **3 × 3 normalized mean in linear power**, preserve invalid centers, then compute **dB = 10 × log10(power)**. Change is **after dB − before dB**.

At or below the selected threshold is a **water candidate**. Both dates below: persistent candidate. Crossing downward: new candidate. Crossing upward: receded candidate. Transitions must also meet the absolute-change control. Other valid pixels remain other surface; missing pixels are always unknown. Areas count projected 100 m² cells.

**The threshold is exploratory, not universal.** Existing channels, smooth ground and radar shadow can produce dark returns. Wind, vegetation, soil moisture and acquisition conditions affect interpretation. Filtering blurs narrow features and has reduced support near AOI boundaries. Isolated detections, scene-wide darkening and subpixel registration need further study. Historical water maps cannot establish flooding on these dates.
